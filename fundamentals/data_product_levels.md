# Product Levels and Interpretation

Remote sensing datasets are often described by processing level:

- **Level 1:** Calibrated radiances or brightness temperatures measured by the sensor
- **Level 2:** Retrieved geophysical variables, such as SST, at the native swath resolution
- **Level 3:** Gridded observations, often composited in space or time
- **Level 4:** Merged and/or interpolated products that may include gap filling

For many oceanographic applications, Level 4 SST products are convenient because they provide complete maps. However, they should be interpreted differently than direct satellite retrievals.
